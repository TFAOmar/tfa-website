Rise Estate Planning — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Who we are

Rise Estate Planning ("we," "us," "our") works with The Financial Architects to help homeowners understand their estate planning options. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information submitted through tfawealthplanning.com/rise, including by phone, email, and SMS text message.

Information we collect

  • Contact information: name, phone number, and email address.
  • The name of your real estate agent.
  • Your answers to the planning questionnaire and your preferred contact method.
  • Your choice on the consent checkbox, including consent to receive text messages.
  • Records of our communications with you, including SMS text messages.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your request and contact you by the method you choose.
  • To send text messages about the information you requested, if you consented.
  • To schedule appointments and follow up on your planning questions.
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations.

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in, Rise Estate Planning may send you text messages about the information you requested. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All of the information-sharing categories in this policy exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

We do not sell or share your information for marketing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their marketing purposes.

When we share information

We share information only as needed to respond to your request: with The Financial Architects, which works with us on this program, and with service providers that help us operate (such as our customer relationship and messaging systems), who must keep it confidential. We may also disclose information when required by law. None of this sharing includes SMS opt-in data or consent, as stated above.

Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. To ask about, update, or delete your information, contact us below.
